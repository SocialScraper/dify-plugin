# Privacy policy

This plugin connects Dify to the SocialScrape API at `https://api.socialscrape.dev`, operated by The Casey Company LLC.

## What the plugin sends

- The inputs you give a tool: video links, account handles, the platform, and options such as a language or a paging cursor.
- Your SocialScrape API key, only as an `x-api-key` header, only to `https://api.socialscrape.dev`.

It sends nothing to any other destination.

## What the plugin stores

Nothing. The plugin does not write files, keep logs, cache responses, or store your API key. Dify stores the API key you enter in its own encrypted credential store.

## What SocialScrape collects

To answer a request and count its credits, SocialScrape logs the request (the endpoint, the input, the outcome, the credits used and the time) against your team. Request logs are kept for 90 days. Results are cached so a repeated request is fast, for at most 30 days, and then deleted automatically. SocialScrape does not sell customer data. When a video has no captions, its audio is transcribed by SocialScrape's speech recognition to produce the transcript. The full privacy policy is at https://socialscrape.dev/privacy.

## Third parties

The profiles, posts and transcripts come from the social platforms named in each tool. Dify, and any model provider your workflow passes the results to, process them under their own policies.
